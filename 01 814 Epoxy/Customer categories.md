# Customer categories

Part of [[814 Epoxy]].

## Problem

QuickBooks and the sales sheets use Commercial / Residential / Industrial (the `R / I / C` column in Customer Leads and `R/I/C` in the Contract Tracker), and nearly everything non-residential defaults to Commercial. Most of that work is really for nonprofits and public bodies, which buy differently from businesses.

## Categories (adopted by Joe 2026-10-16)

| Category | Who | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Residential | homeowners | garages, basements |
| Commercial | for-profit businesses | shops, restaurants, showrooms |
| Institutional | nonprofits | hospitals, senior centers, mental health associations, churches |
| Public | government bodies | city and county buildings, schools, municipal fire departments |
| Industrial | manufacturing, warehouse | plants, production floors |

Fire departments go under Public (Joe's call).

## Why it matters

- **Selling:** nonprofits and public bodies buy on budget cycles, boards, grants and sometimes bids. Facility managers in this network refer each other, which fits word-of-mouth growth.
- **Getting paid:** slower approvals and different paperwork (POs, W-9s, tax-exempt certificates).
- **Tax:** how exempt customers affect sales tax on materials and invoices is a question for SKM before any change is made in QuickBooks.
- **Knowing the business:** the real mix shows where the money comes from.
