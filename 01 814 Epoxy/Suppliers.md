# Suppliers

Part of [[814 Epoxy]]. Who 814 buys from and the people there.

## Sherwin-Williams

Two accounts:

- **814 Epoxy** – all flooring and epoxy purchases. Served by the flooring rep out of Pittsburgh.
- **814 Paint and More** – paint purchases. Credited to Jake's sales. Eric runs discounted deals through it, and other customers he sends sometimes buy on it with their own cards, which builds the account's volume.

People:

- **Eric Albrecht** – Erie store manager, about 35. One of Joe's closest professional contacts; they talk daily. Gets Joe deals and keeps him in the loop on Sherwin news.
- **Jake Anderson** – Sherwin's regional commercial painting sales rep, about 35. Played offensive line at Akron. Tight with Joe and Eric. Not 814's flooring rep (Sherwin assigns flooring to Pittsburgh), but Joe wishes he were; he'll do anything for 814.
- **Chase Coons** – 814's flooring rep, based in Pittsburgh. Good rep, but far from the day-to-day.

## Niagara

- **Matt Lefevre** – runs Niagara's Erie branch (formerly its headquarters). Close with Joe; they talk pricing often.
- **Alex Trahorova** – Niagara rep. Close with Justin. Helped early with demos and product questions; 814 has since outgrown needing that.
