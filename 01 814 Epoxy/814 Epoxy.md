# 814 Epoxy

814 Epoxy and More, based in Erie, PA. Read this note and everything it links to before working on anything for 814.

## What it is

- Epoxy and resin floor coatings. Rough mix of work: 77% "commercial", 18% residential, 5% industrial.
- Much of the "commercial" work is actually **nonprofit and public**: hospitals, senior centers, fire departments, mental health associations. About 40% of property in the city of Erie is nonprofit. See [[Customer categories]].
- Typical job about $7–8k on a weighted average. A garage averages about $3,500; a commercial job is closer to $10k.
- Area: mostly Western PA, roughly the Buffalo–Pittsburgh–Cleveland triangle. Has traveled for the right price (Altoona, Marietta OH) and would take more out-of-town work.
- **Leads are all word of mouth.** No paid advertising beyond an occasional ~$50 Facebook ad.
