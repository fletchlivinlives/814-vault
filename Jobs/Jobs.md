# Jobs

Playbooks for repeat work. Each job is a folder with an index note of the same name, built from [[Job]]. When Joe names a task that matches one, read it and follow it.

- [[Morning office check]] – 814: weekday-morning read-only summary of money owed, cold leads and the schedule, sent to Joe's phone.
- [[Lead follow-ups]] – 814: work the overdue leads, biggest bids first; Gmail drafts for Joe to send.
- [[Money in]] – 814: recording a payment or deposit: QuickBooks first, then the Contract Tracker, then the bank deposit.
